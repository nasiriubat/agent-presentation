CloudNote 4 review: fast, private, a little pricey

By Jamie Ortega · Updated 2026-09-14 · 6 min read

CloudNote 4 is the best note-taking app we tested this year. Sync is near-instant, search finds text inside PDFs and photos, and end-to-end encryption is now on by default.

Downsides: the Pro plan went up to $12/month, and the Android app still lags behind iOS.

Verdict: 8.5/10. Great for teams that care about privacy.
